About / Manifesto

MAKE
ROOM.

BOUNJAH. is a visual archive at the intersection of graphic design, editorial experimentation, image-making and body-hair-positive art.

It treats body hair as a natural part of human variation—not a flaw to correct or a spectacle. Expect strong typography, poster studies, photography, zines, process notes and thoughtful images that celebrate real bodies with care.
