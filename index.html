About / Manifesto

FOLLICLE. is a visual archive at the intersection of graphic design, editorial experimentation, image-making and body-hair-positive art.

It treats body hair as a natural part of human variation—not a flaw to correct or a spectacle. Expect strong typography, poster studies, photography, zines, process notes and thoughtful images that celebrate real bodies with care.

Community standards: Publish only content you have the rights and permission to share. Respect privacy and consent, and follow applicable laws and your hosting platform's rules.
